    Current conclusion

    Do not pick a memory product from one headline score.

    Fix the dataset, budget, reader and judge. Then inspect what was lost at ingestion, retrieval and reading before deciding whether complexity paid for itself.

    Public registry

    Every claim points to a bounded run.

    Protocol before product

    A sovereign benchmark can rebuild every derived view.

    Raw records are canonical. Chunks, embeddings, facts, graphs and indexes are replaceable artifacts.

    01

    Common data

    Keep immutable source records and version expected facts, dates, conflicts, provenance and abstention cases.

    02

    Common budget

    Compare words or tokens, not only top-k. Record index, ingestion, retrieval, reader and judge costs separately.

    03

    Common contracts

    Adapters expose ingest, retrieve, construct-context, answer and trace. Vendor-specific details stay behind the contract.

    04

    Common evidence

    Repeat runs, group correlated samples, keep raw hashes and distinguish proxy metrics from final task quality.
